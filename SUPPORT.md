# Support

Need help or have a question?

- **It will not compile:** check the compiler is XeLaTeX or LuaLaTeX first, as the [README](README.md#build-it) explains, then open an issue with the error message.
- **Ideas and improvements:** open an issue.
- **More about the files:** [README.md](README.md).

> [!TIP]
> Reach out directly at [contact@isaacadjei.me](mailto:contact@isaacadjei.me) or through [isaacadjei.me/contact](https://isaacadjei.me/contact).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never post a real CV with personal details in an issue. Reduce the problem to a few lines of example text.
